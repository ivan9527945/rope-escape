# Rope Escape 隱私政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-10

## 中文

Rope Escape 不收集、不儲存、也不傳送任何個人資料。

- App 完全離線運作，不連網、不使用定位、不含廣告、不含分析或追蹤 SDK、不含第三方登入或付款服務。
- 遊戲紀錄（最佳高度、場次、最佳連擊、最長時間）只以 iOS 的本機偏好設定儲存在你的裝置上，不會離開裝置；刪除 App 即一併刪除。
- App 啟動時會把合成好的音效暫存在裝置的暫存目錄，僅供播放使用。
- 本 App 不針對 13 歲以下兒童蒐集任何資訊。

如有疑問請來信 ivan78594785@gmail.com。

## English

Rope Escape does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- The app works fully offline. It makes no network requests, does not use location, and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, third-party sign-in or payment services.
- Game records (best height, runs, best combo, longest run) are kept only on your device using iOS local preferences and are deleted together with the app.
- Synthesized sound files are written to the device's temporary folder at launch solely for playback.
- The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
