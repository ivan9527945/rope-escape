About Rope Escape

ROPE
ESCAPE

A one-thumb rope-climbing action game

Soft Verlet ropes, swing momentum, random anchors and traps, and a floor that never stops rising. Swing, let go, catch the next rope and see how high you can climb.

  • Tap to jump: the game checks whether you can reach a higher rope
  • Saw wheels, snapping ropes, shields and pickups, randomized every run
  • Offline, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected
  • Sound and music are synthesized at launch and speed up with the tension

ROPE ESCAPE — HOW TO PLAY
=========================

Goal
  Hang on a rope, swing, and jump to higher ropes
  to stay ahead of the floor rising from below.
  The higher you climb, the higher the score.
  1500 m and above is an S rank.

Touch / mouse
  Tap .............. Jump (auto-catches next rope)
  Hold ............. Pump: swing harder, wears rope
  Swipe down ....... Lower: lengthen the rope
  Swipe up/side .... Jump at once, no assist
  II (top right) ... Pause

Keyboard (desktop / browser)
  Space or ↑ ....... Jump
  ↓ ................ Lower
  Hold Shift / ← → . Pump
  Esc or P ......... Pause / resume
  Enter ............ Start / play again

Tips
  * When JUMP turns green, letting go will
    always catch a higher rope.
  * Pressing a little early is fine: the jump
    waits for the green light.
  * Saw wheels cut the rope; a shield blocks
    one hit.
  * The browser version keeps records on this
    device only. Nothing is uploaded.
